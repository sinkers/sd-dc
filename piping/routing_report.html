Terminology

Plant
Mechanical equipment with fluid connections — pumps, heat exchangers, tanks, CDUs, dry coolers.
Nozzle
A connection point on plant defined by position (centre of face) and direction (unit vector pointing outward). Pipes must align collinearly with the nozzle axis.
Piping
Straight pipe segments and 90° elbows connecting plant outlets to plant inlets. All runs are orthogonal.
Obstacle
Any object the pipe must avoid — columns, cable trays, platforms, other pipes.

Routing Rules

  1. Outlet → Inlet only — every pipe run connects one plant outlet to one plant inlet. No other connections are valid.
  2. Nozzle alignment — first segment departs in outlet’s outward direction; last segment arrives opposite to inlet’s outward direction.
  3. 90° elbows only — no 180° reversals. Direction reversal requires two turns with a perpendicular intermediate.
  4. Minimum segment length — interior segments ≥ 2×BR (343 mm); end segments ≥ 1×BR (171.5 mm).
  5. Chain integrity — every segment exit = next segment entry (0.000 mm gap).

Route Around + Route Over

Objective

Route supply and return piping between a pump and heat exchanger separated by a full-height column (must route around horizontally) and a cable tray (cleared vertically by pipe elevation).

Plant & Obstacles

  • Pump — outlet (+X face, green), inlet (−X face, magenta)
  • Heat Exchanger — inlet (−X face, magenta), outlet (+X face, green)
  • Column — full-height obstacle at X=1600–2000 (orange)
  • Cable Tray — low obstacle at Z=0–150 (orange)
Front (XZ) — red return rises to Z=1700 over column
Top (XY) — blue supply detours −Y around column

Routing Strategy

  1. Supply (blue, pump outlet → HX inlet): Departs +X, elbows −Y to bypass column, traverses +X past column footprint, elbows +Y back to centreline, arrives at HX inlet.
  2. Return (red, HX outlet → pump inlet): Departs +X, rises +Z to 1700 mm (above column top at 1500), traverses −X at elevation, drops −Z, arrives +X at pump inlet.

Parallel Pump Manifold

Objective

SCENARIO D — FAIL (expected)

Blocked Outlet — Obstacle Too Close

Objective

Demonstrate that routing correctly rejects a layout where an obstacle is placed within the minimum departure distance of a nozzle. The pipe cannot create a valid first segment.

Layout

  • Pump — outlet nozzle at X=500, direction +X (green)
  • Blocker — obstacle face at X=600 (red), only 100 mm from nozzle
  • Heat Exchanger — destination with inlet (magenta), unreachable
ERROR: Cannot depart nozzle — obstacle at 100mm, minimum clearance is 171.5mm (1×BR)
Route validation failed: first segment length (100mm) < bend radius (171.5mm)

Why It Fails

The first pipe segment must travel in the nozzle’s outward direction (+X) for at least one bend radius (171.5 mm) before the first elbow can begin. With the obstacle face at only 100 mm, there is no room for a valid departure. The router correctly rejects this layout.

Enclosed Inlet — No Valid Approach Path

Objective

Demonstrate that routing correctly rejects a layout where the destination inlet is enclosed by obstacles on all approach axes. No orthogonal path can arrive from the required direction.

Layout

  • Pump — outlet nozzle (green), clear departure
  • Heat Exchanger — inlet nozzle facing −X (magenta), surrounded by walls (semi-transparent red)
  • Enclosure — walls block approach from −X, +Y, −Y, and +Z. Floor blocks −Z.
ERROR: No valid approach to inlet — all orthogonal paths blocked
Required arrival direction: +X (opposite of inlet’s −X outward direction)
Approach from −X: BLOCKED (front wall)
Approach from ±Y: BLOCKED (side walls)
Approach from +Z: BLOCKED (top wall)
Approach from −Z: BLOCKED (floor)

Why It Fails

The pipe must arrive at the inlet traveling in +X direction (opposite to the inlet’s −X outward direction). To approach in +X, the preceding segment must come from somewhere at X<2500 — but the front wall at X=2200–2300 blocks this path. All perpendicular approaches (which would require an elbow to turn into +X) are also blocked by the side and top walls. The router correctly determines no valid route exists.
